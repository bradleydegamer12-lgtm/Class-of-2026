Timetable

📖 How to use this tab

›

Your weekly class schedule is displayed below with exact times for each period.

  • 1Today's timetable is automatically highlighted with a green border and "Today" badge.
  • 2Fridays alternate between 1st Friday and 2nd Friday schedules — the app detects which one it is.
  • 3Lunch breaks appear in grey and After School periods appear in green.
Notes

📖 How to share notes

›

Share class notes with your classmates and teachers. Everyone can contribute!

  • 1Tap the green + button to create a new note. Choose a subject, write a title, and paste your notes.
  • 2Toggle "Teacher's note" if you're sharing official teacher notes.
  • 3After saving, share via WhatsApp, Copy, Download as a file, or generate a QR code for classmates to scan.
  • 4Use the subject filter buttons above the notes to find notes for a specific subject.
